How predictions work

1

Learn team strengths

Dixon-Coles MLE estimates attack and defence parameters for every team from historical results. Updated weekly.

2

Compute expected goals

Your attack vs their defence, adjusted for home advantage, injuries, and form. Real interaction, not vibes.

3

Simulate 15,000 matches

Monte Carlo draws random goals from probability distributions. Produces real win/draw/loss percentages and scoreline probabilities.

4

The gaffer explains

Llama 3.3 narrates the model outputs using FPL data: player form, injuries, xG, set pieces. The model computes. The AI explains.

Accuracy tracking

Every prediction stored and resolved. See your outcome accuracy, exact score rate, streaks, and confidence calibration.

Data logged weekly

FPL player form, injuries, team strength, and fixture results captured every gameweek. A dataset no external API provides.

Full league coverage

Premier League with FPL-enriched data. La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, and Champions League via football-data.org.
